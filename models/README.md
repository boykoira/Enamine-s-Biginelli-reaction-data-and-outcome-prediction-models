# SVM classification models and associated files

 *libsvm* models were developed on ISIDA descriptors using GA for selection of hyperparameters and descriptor spaces. The appropriate .hdr and .xml files for descriptor calculations are provided.
 To gain insight into the descriptor nomenclature, please refer to the [manual](https://infochim.u-strasbg.fr/downloads/manuals/ISIDADescriptors/Nomenclature_of_ISIDA_fragments_2015.pdf). You can request a copy of ISIDA Fragmentor at this [link](https://infochim.chimie.unistra.fr/-DOWNLOADS-SOFTWARE-.html). 

 The Biginelli product core substucture is provided to access the applicability of these models to your compounds. 

 For the usage of *libsvm* models for predictions, please refer to the  *libsvm* [documentation](https://github.com/cjlin1/libsvm/tree/master), specifically, *svm-predict*.

Other models, used in this work, may be available upon request. Please contact the corresponding authors of the [paper](https://chemrxiv.org/doi/full/10.26434/chemrxiv.15000783/v1) for any further questions.
